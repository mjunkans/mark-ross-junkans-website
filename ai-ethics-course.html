Frequently Asked Questions

I'm not tech-savvy. Will I be able to follow this?

Absolutely. This course assumes zero technical knowledge. It's designed for pastors, not programmers. If you can watch a video and read a workbook, you're good.

How long will it take to complete?

The videos are 95 minutes total (about 1.5 hours). Watch all at once or spread it out. The workbook adds another 2-3 hours if you do all the reflection questions. Most pastors complete it in one week.

Is this specific to a certain denomination?

The course is broadly Protestant but applicable across traditions. The theological framework (imago Dei, incarnation, creation mandate) is ecumenical. Examples are adapted to various contexts.

Will the course be updated as AI changes?

Yes. The Complete version includes lifetime updates. As AI evolves, new modules, case studies, and resources will be added. You'll be notified when updates are available.

Can I use this to train my staff or leadership?

Yes. The Complete version includes PowerPoint slides and teaching guides for presenting to your team. Email mjunkans71@gmail.com for group pricing (5+ seats).

Good. The goal isn't for you to agree with everything. The goal is to equip you to think theologically about AI. Use the framework, adapt it to your context, and make it your own.
