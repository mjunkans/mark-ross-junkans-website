6 Core Databases (Fully Built)

1. Sermons Database

Track sermon ideas, research, outlines, manuscripts. Calendar view of preaching schedule. Kanban board (Idea to Final). Searchable archive by topic or date.

2. Tasks Database

All church tasks in one place. Filter by priority, due date, category. Recurring task automation. Link to events, sermons, or members. Overdue alerts.

3. Members Database

Complete member directory with contact info. Birthdays, anniversaries, join dates. Pastoral care notes (private, searchable). Track small groups and serving roles. Visitor follow-up queue.

4. Events Database

Calendar of all church events. Track attendance, budget, volunteers needed. Link events to sermons, tasks, and volunteers. Post-event review notes. Searchable archive.

5. Budget Tracker

Income and expenses in one view. Track by category (missions, staff, building, ministry). Year-to-date totals. Compare budget vs. actual. Receipt uploads.

6. Volunteer Management

Who's serving when. Track recurring roles (worship, children's, hospitality). Training completion tracking. Confirmation sent checkboxes. Link volunteers to events.

Frequently Asked Questions

I've never used Notion. Will this be too complicated?

There is a learning curve to Notion, but the template does most of the work for you. The setup instructions walk you through everything step-by-step. If you can use Google Sheets, you can use this. That said, if you've never opened Notion before, budget 1-2 hours to get comfortable.

Do I need a paid Notion account?

Not necessarily. Notion's free plan works for solo pastors. If you have a team (staff, volunteers), you'll want Notion's Team plan ($8-10/user/month). The template works with either.

Can I customize the template for my church's specific needs?

Absolutely. The template is fully customizable. Add fields, change views, rename things. It's yours. The setup guide includes tips for common customizations.

What if my church uses a different system (Planning Center, ChurchTrac, etc.)?

This template is best for churches that don't already have a robust church management system (ChMS). If you're happy with your current system, you probably don't need this. But if you're frustrated with piecemeal tools or expensive ChMS subscriptions, this is a great alternative.

Can multiple staff members use this template?

Yes. You can share the Notion workspace with your staff. Each person can have their own views and filters. Note: Notion charges per user for teams (after the first 10 guests on the free plan).

How do I get the template?

After purchase, you'll receive a link to duplicate the template into your own Notion workspace. It takes about 5 minutes to duplicate, then 20-30 minutes to customize with your church's info.

Will this integrate with my church's giving platform or website?

Not directly. Notion has limited integrations compared to dedicated ChMS platforms. This template is best for internal church management, not public-facing tools. You can manually input data or use Zapier for basic automation.

What if Notion changes and breaks the template?

If you purchase the Complete version, you get lifetime updates. If Notion makes breaking changes, I'll update the template and notify you.
